# Privacy Policy — Loan Tracker

**Last updated:** 22 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Loan Tracker** mobile app ("the App", "we", "us")
handles information about you. The App is operated by **Nojus Sargevičius**
(contact: **dunojus10@gmail.com**).

By using the App, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### From Google Sign-In
When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

- Your Google account user ID
- Your email address
- Your display name
- Your profile picture URL

This information is stored in our Firebase project so we can identify you across sessions
and so other users can address loans to you by your email.

### Loan data you create
When you create or accept a loan, the App stores:

- The lender's and borrower's email addresses, names, and user IDs
- The loan amount (principal), one-time interest multiplier, currency
- Due date and timestamps (created, accepted, paid, deleted)
- The "default consequence if not paid" text and any optional notes you add
- The loan's status (pending, active, paid, overdue, declined, deleted)

### Saved contacts
When you create a loan, the recipient's email is stored under your private contacts list
in our database so the App can suggest them when you create future loans.

### What we do **not** collect
- We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or your location.
- We do not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not access your contacts list, photos, microphone, camera, or any other device
  data.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information solely to:

- Authenticate you and keep you signed in
- Display your loans to you and to the other party of each loan
- Compute the totals and next-deadline reminders shown in the App
- Persist your "Show loan history" preference

We do **not** sell or share your personal data with anyone outside of the service
providers described below.

---

## 3. Who can see your data

- **You** see your own data inside the App at all times.
- **The other party of each loan** sees the full content of that loan: the amount,
  interest multiplier, due date, default-consequence text, optional note, both names,
  and both email addresses. Do not write anything in those fields that you wouldn't
  want them to read.
- **Google / Firebase** provides the underlying infrastructure. Firebase Authentication
  and Cloud Firestore store and transmit the data on our behalf. Google's privacy
  practices apply to that processing:
  - <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>
  - <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

---

## 4. Data retention

- A loan record is kept until it is deleted by the lender. Deleting a loan marks it as
  **Deleted** — the record remains visible to both parties in the history view but
  no longer counts toward active totals.
- Your profile (email, name, photo URL) is retained while your account exists.
- Server-side backups managed by Google may retain copies for a limited period after
  deletion in line with Google's standard policies.

---

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right under laws such as the GDPR
(EU/UK) or CCPA (California) to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Receive a portable copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email **dunojus10@gmail.com**. We will respond
within a reasonable time and within any statutory deadline that applies to your
jurisdiction.

### Deleting your account
To delete your account and the data attached to it, contact us at the address above.
Note that loans you created or accepted are also stored on the other party's account;
deleting your account removes your profile and your private contacts, but other parties
may retain their copy of shared loan records.

---

## 6. Security

Data in transit between the App and our database is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data at
rest is stored in Google's Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard access
controls. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

---

## 7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic
Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child
has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
